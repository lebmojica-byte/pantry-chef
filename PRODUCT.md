# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The owner, family and friends. They open it at the fridge on a phone or plan a meal on a laptop,
equally often. The job: turn what is already in the kitchen into something to cook tonight.

## Product Purpose
Keep a living list of fridge, pantry, spices and fresh herbs, pick diet and type of dish, and get
three AI recipes that use what is there. Success: fewer "what's for dinner?" stand-offs, less food
thrown away, and people coming back because it is fun to use.

## Brand Personality
Playful, fun to tap, warm. Every tap answers with a small physical response. Grown-up playful,
like a well-made kitchen gadget, never a children's app.

## Anti-references
- Generic recipe blogs: ad-and-card layouts, stock food photos, beige everything.
- Corporate SaaS: dashboard feel, blue-grey, enterprise chrome.
- Childish cartoon: mascots, comic fonts, over-rounded toy UI.
- Dark techy: black hacker-tool look, neon accents.

## Design Principles
1. Tapping is the fun: adding an ingredient or picking a dish should feel like pressing a real button.
2. Kitchen first: the shelves and what's on them are the hero; recipes are the reward.
3. One hand, one glance: big targets, short labels, works at the fridge with wet hands.
4. Honest about the AI: show what's missing ("to buy") instead of pretending.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast in light and dark, visible focus, full keyboard use,
44px touch targets, prefers-reduced-motion respected (transforms off, fades kept).
